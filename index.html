Segmented journeys for student leads

ExcelR, 2025 to now

60%conversion in 6 months
The goal
Convert and re-engage the final and semi-final-year student database through targeted messaging.
What I did
Segmented the database in Salesforce and built WebEngage journeys across email, WhatsApp, and RCS. A/B tested subject lines, CTA placement, and message formats on every send. Ran a personalised Christmas 2025 campaign and a Refer & Earn campaign with automated follow-ups.
The result
60% conversion on new leads, 52% on the Christmas campaign, and 35% on Refer & Earn. A dead-lead reactivation campaign also converted 2% of a segment that had stopped responding.
